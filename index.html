iConnect: your online worlds, one home

iConnect is a free social platform where you can share posts, chat privately, join communities, go live, buy and sell on the marketplace, find jobs, book stays and rides, meet people, save with Ajo plans and get help from an AI assistant, all with one account.

What you can do on iConnect

  • Social feed, stories, private chat, communities, live video and a video hub
  • iConnect Market, homes and local services, jobs, iBook stays and rides, and ads for businesses
  • iConnect Ajo savings plans, practice FX trading and Premium publisher plans
  • iConnect AI assistant
